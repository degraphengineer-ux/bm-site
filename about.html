Our Story

Capturing the heart of Turkana through cinematic motion.

A Journey of Light & Connection

Born from a passion for visual storytelling, M.I Photography was established with a singular mission: to capture the raw, unscripted beauty of the human experience. Fiston Apungu, a visionary artist based in Turkana, Kenya, founded the studio to provide a creative sanctuary where moments are transformed into timeless art.

"I believe that every person has a story that deserves to be told with dignity and elegance," says Fiston. "At BM Studio, we don't just provide a service; we build a connection. We want you to look at your photographs years from now and feel exactly what you felt in that moment."
